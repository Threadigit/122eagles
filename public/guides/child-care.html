Resource guide

Look for child care that fits your schedule

Use Maryland Family Network’s referral service to begin a search around your family’s needs.

Sources checked November 4, 2026

Who this is for
Parents and caregivers searching for regulated child care in Maryland.
Where it applies
Maryland · search by location

LOCATE: Child Care is Maryland Family Network’s free, confidential referral service. It helps families search for care using considerations such as location, schedule and cost.

Search with LOCATE: Child Care ↗

Takes you to Maryland Family Network.

Your next steps

  1. Open LOCATE and use its online search or contact the referral service.
  2. Prepare your child’s age, the hours you need, your travel area and your budget.
  3. Contact providers directly to ask about openings, hours, fees and the next step in visiting or applying.

A question to take with you

Do you have an opening for my child’s age, and can you cover my actual work and travel hours?

Before you rely on it

The referral service is free; child care itself may have fees. Search results do not guarantee an opening, affordability or a particular fit for your child.

Source: Maryland Family Network. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
