Find a local starting point for care and support

Connect with Maryland Access Point for aging, disability and caregiver resources.

Sources checked November 4, 2026

Who this is for
Older adults, people with disabilities and their caregivers in Maryland.
Where it applies
Maryland · local MAP offices

Maryland Access Point helps older adults, people with disabilities and caregivers identify services and plan for support. The Department of Aging lists local offices and explains the available assistance.

Find your local MAP office ↗

Takes you to Maryland Department of Aging.

Your next steps

  1. Find the MAP office for the person’s county on the Department of Aging page.
  2. Describe the practical support needed, such as meals, transportation or help planning care.
  3. Ask about options counseling, local referrals and the application requirements for any suggested service.

A question to take with you

Which local services fit these needs, and who should we contact to understand cost and availability?

Before you rely on it

A referral does not guarantee immediate care or a funded place. Individual programs may have their own eligibility rules, costs and waiting lists.

Source: Maryland Department of Aging. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
