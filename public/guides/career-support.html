Talk to someone about your next job

Find free job-search support and training referrals through a Maryland American Job Center.

Who this is for
Job seekers, career changers and Marylanders exploring training.
Where it applies
Maryland · county directory

Where to start

Maryland’s American Job Centers offer free employment services, including résumé help, career exploration and referrals to training. The Department of Labor’s directory lets you find a center by county.

Find your county’s job center

Takes you to Maryland Department of Labor.

Your next steps

  1. Find the center serving your county in the official directory.
  2. Check its contact details and ask whether you need an appointment for the service you want.
  3. Explain your work experience, goals and scheduling constraints. Ask which training or job-search options fit.

A question to take with you

I need to keep earning while I look for better work. Which services or training routes could fit my schedule?

Before you rely on it

A referral is not a job offer or a promise of training funding. Confirm the requirements and costs of any course before enrolling.

Source: Maryland Department of Labor. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
