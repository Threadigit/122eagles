Get another set of eyes on your business

Find confidential, no-cost business consulting through the Maryland SBDC.

Where to start

The Maryland Small Business Development Center offers no-cost, confidential consulting to prospective and existing business owners. It can be a useful place to discuss a business plan, market questions or a growth challenge.

Explore SBDC consulting

Takes you to Maryland Small Business Development Center.

Your next steps

  1. Visit the SBDC consulting page and follow its process for requesting assistance.
  2. Write down the decision you need help making and the main constraint facing your business.
  3. Ask what information to bring, which advisor is appropriate and when an appointment is available.

A question to take with you

What should I examine first to understand why this part of my business is not working?

Before you rely on it

Consulting does not guarantee customers, financing or a particular business outcome. Confirm any separate training or third-party costs before committing.
