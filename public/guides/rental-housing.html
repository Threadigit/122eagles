Start your search for rental housing support

State resources for finding a rental, exploring assistance and locating renter information.

Who this is for
Maryland renters and people looking for rental housing.
Where it applies
Maryland · local programs vary
Sources checked
November 4, 2026

Where to start

Maryland’s housing department brings rental listings, assistance programs and tenant information together on its renter resources page. The right route depends on whether you need a property, financial support or information about a tenancy.

Explore Maryland renter resources

Takes you to Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development.

Your next steps

  1. Use the state’s renter resources page to choose the issue closest to yours.
  2. For a rental search, follow MD Housing Search. For voucher information, use the local public housing authority route described on the page.
  3. Ask the relevant provider about current availability, eligibility, application steps and waiting lists.

A question to take with you

Does this service cover my county, and is it accepting applications or referrals now?

Before you rely on it

A listing or referral is not a housing offer. Program availability varies. For a legal notice or deadline, seek qualified help promptly rather than relying on a general housing search.

Source: Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
