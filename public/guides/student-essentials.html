Find your university’s basic-needs resources

A starting point for campus food and essential-resource support across the University System of Maryland.

Sources checked November 4, 2026

Who this is for
Students at University System of Maryland institutions.
Where it applies
USM institutions · campus eligibility

The University System of Maryland maintains an essential-resources directory with links to campus food pantries and other support. Use the listing for your institution to find the current local service.

Find your campus resource ↗

Takes you to University System of Maryland.

Your next steps

  1. Find your university in the USM essential-resources directory.
  2. Open the campus resource page and check who can use the service, its hours and any registration requirements.
  3. If your college is outside USM, ask its student affairs office which basic-needs services it offers.

A question to take with you

What support can I use now, and is there a separate contact for needs this service does not cover?

Before you rely on it

This directory focuses on USM institutions, not every Maryland college. Availability and eligibility are set by each campus; a listed resource is not a guarantee of funding.

Source: University System of Maryland. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
