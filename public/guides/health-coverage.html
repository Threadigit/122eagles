Where to start

Maryland Health Connection’s help page connects residents with enrollment assistance. Local navigators offer free help with coverage options and questions about eligibility.

Find local enrollment help

Takes you to Maryland Health Connection.

Prefer to call? 1-855-642-8572 is Maryland Health Connection’s consumer support number.

Your next steps

  1. Open the official Find Help page and choose a local navigator or another listed assistance route.
  2. Explain whether you are applying, changing coverage or trying to understand an existing application.
  3. Ask about enrollment timing, required documents and the costs of any coverage you are considering.

A question to take with you

Which options can I apply for in my situation, and what would I pay for the coverage and care?

Before you rely on it

Free enrollment assistance does not mean every health plan is free. Eligibility, enrollment windows and costs depend on your circumstances. This guide concerns coverage, not medical treatment.

Source: Maryland Health Connection. Provider information can change. Confirm current details with the service. MD Eagles is a publication, not the service provider.
