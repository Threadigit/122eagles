About MD Eagles

Maryland has people ready to make things better. We help them find a useful place to start.

Maryland Eagles Eyes is an independent publication about problems facing people in our state and practical ways to help solve them. A resident organizing neighbors, a student starting a project, a business owner or a founder should be able to find work worth taking on here.

From a problem to a first step

Our problem briefs bring together published evidence, the people affected, possible solutions and a small local starting point. A solution might be a service, a neighborhood effort, a better process or a tool. It does not have to be an app.

We label our proposals as ideas to test. They are not proven interventions, funded projects or open roles. Named organizations are sources or possible places to learn, not partners unless we explicitly say so.

Evidence before promises

Each brief links to its sources and explains where the evidence applies. We distinguish published research from our own proposals. These first briefs are based on existing reports; they are not original interviews with residents. Local conversations should shape any project before it starts.

Help for what you are facing today

Our resource guides point readers to existing Maryland services. The problem collection is for people who want to work on the gaps.
