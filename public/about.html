About MD Eagles

Maryland has people ready to make things better. We help them find a useful place to start.

Baltimore’s Inner Harbor · Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress

Maryland Eagles Eyes is an independent publication about problems facing people in our state and practical ways to help solve them. A resident organizing neighbors, a student starting a project, a business owner or a founder should be able to find work worth taking on here.

Two ways in

The problem collection is for gaps that are still open: documented problems with possible solutions that people can test. The resource guides cover problems that already have an answer, found by county or by what is going on in your life.

From a problem to a first step

Each brief pairs published evidence with possible solutions and a small local starting point. A solution might be a service, a neighborhood effort or a better process. It does not have to be an app.

Our proposals are ideas to test, not proven interventions or open roles. Named organizations are sources, not partners, unless we say so.

Evidence before promises

Each brief links its sources and says where the evidence applies. These first briefs draw on existing reports, not interviews with residents, so local conversations should shape any project before it starts.

Independent

MD Eagles is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the State of Maryland or any agency. We link to the agency that runs each service.
